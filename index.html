About YooHoo!

State-of-the-Art Facility

Welcome to YooHoo! Trampoline & Amusement Park

YooHoo! Trampoline & Amusement Park is Sri Ganganagar's most exciting indoor fun destination where kids, teenagers, and families can enjoy thrilling trampoline jumping, engaging games, spectacular birthday parties, and unforgettable adventures in a completely safe and energetic environment.

With state-of-the-art facilities, trained staff, and a commitment to safety, we create memories that last a lifetime!
